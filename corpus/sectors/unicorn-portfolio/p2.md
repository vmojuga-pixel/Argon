## Newton-Raphson Sukuk YTM Solver
Implied Yield-to-Maturity for Islamic bond pricing.
Iterate: pv = sum(coupon/(1+r)^t) + parValue/(1+r)^periods
Derivative: dpv = -sum(t*coupon/(1+r)^(t+1))
Update: ytm -= (pv - marketPrice) / dpv
Converge: abs(diff) < 1e-6, max 60 iterations.
Frequency: semi-annual (freq=2). Compute Macaulay Duration after YTM.
