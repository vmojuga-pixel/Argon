# RAS Water Quality Digital Twin + Auto-Control
## Problem
Land-based seafood (RAS) sensitif. DO jatuh atau ammonia naik = mati dalam 30 minit. Manual monitoring tak cukup.
## Solution
Proportional control engine: DO rendah → boost aerator + stop feeder. Ammonia tinggi → water exchange. pH out of range → alert.
## Real Source
- Proportional control (control theory)
- RAS best practices (FAO)
- Aquaculture IoT controllers
## Metrics
Response <10s, DO maintained >5.5mg/L, mortality ↓ 40%
