# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-15

## Prohibited Uses

### Illegal Activity
- Violating any local, state, national, or international law
- Facilitating illegal transactions or services

### Harmful Content
- Generating content that promotes violence, terrorism, or self-harm
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — zero tolerance
- Non-consensual intimate imagery

### Abuse
- Spam, phishing, or social engineering
- Bypassing rate limits via multiple accounts
- DoS/DDoS attempts
- Scraping the Service without authorization

### Intellectual Property Violation
- Infringing copyrights, trademarks, or patents
- Generating content that impersonates real people

### Malicious Code
- Using Omni-Overlord to deploy malware
- Crypto mining on our infrastructure
- Botnet coordination

## Enforcement

Violations result in:
1. Warning (minor, first offense)
2. Temporary suspension (repeated)
3. Permanent ban (severe)
4. Legal action (illegal activity)
