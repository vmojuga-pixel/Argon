# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-15

## Cookies We Use

| Cookie | Purpose | Type | Duration |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| `argon_session` | Authentication | Essential | 7 days |

## Cookie Details

### argon_session
- **Purpose:** Keep you signed in
- **Content:** HMAC-signed JSON (user + expiry)
- **Flags:** HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax
- **NOT used for:** tracking, advertising, analytics

## Third-Party Cookies

**None.** We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any tracking service.

## Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in browser settings. This will log you out of the dashboard but API access via `X-API-Key` will still work.
