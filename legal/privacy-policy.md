# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Email address
- Company name (optional)
- Billing information (via payment processor)

### Usage Data
- API requests (endpoint, timestamp, response status)
- Token counts
- IP address
- User agent

### Content Data
- Chat messages and prompts (retained for 30 days by default)
- Generated outputs

## 2. How We Use Information

- **Provide the Service:** Process requests, generate outputs
- **Billing:** Calculate usage, process payments
- **Security:** Detect abuse, prevent fraud
- **Improvement:** Aggregate analytics to improve quality
- **Communication:** Send service updates, security alerts

## 3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your data. We share only with:
- **Cloud providers** (Cloudflare) for infrastructure
- **Payment processors** (Stripe) for billing
- **Legal authorities** when required by law

## 4. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention |
|:---|:---|
| Account info | Until deletion + 30 days |
| Chat messages | 30 days (default) |
| Audit logs | 1 year |
| Billing records | 7 years (legal requirement) |

## 5. Your Rights (GDPR / PDPA)

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data (via dashboard export)
- **Rectify** inaccurate information
- **Erase** your account ("right to be forgotten")
- **Port** your data (JSON export)
- **Object** to processing
- **Restrict** processing

Contact privacy@argon.dev (placeholder) for requests.

## 6. Security

- All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Secrets encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM)
- Audit logs are tamper-proof (SHA-256 hash-chain)
- Access controlled via API keys + role-based access

## 7. International Transfers

Data may be processed in:
- Cloudflare edge network (global)
- Your selected region (for enterprise)

## 8. Cookies

We use minimal cookies:
- **Session cookie** (`argon_session`): authentication, HttpOnly, Secure
- No tracking or advertising cookies

## 9. Children

The Service is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

We will notify you of material changes via email 14 days before they take effect.
